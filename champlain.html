Henri Abraham Chatelain

Carte de la Nouvelle France, ou se voit le cours des grandes rivières

1719 · Amsterdam

Produced in 1719 for Chatelain's Atlas Historique, this map draws on De l'Isle's authoritative 1718 cartography to present French territorial claims at the precise moment the Mississippi Bubble was at its peak.

Published for an audience gripped by Louisiana fever, its rich decoration made French imperial ambition feel vivid, real, and vast to the European readership consuming it.

Chatelain was born to a French Huguenot family exiled to Amsterdam — a refugee from French royal power producing the era's most elaborate image of French imperial reach. The irony is one of the more remarkable footnotes of the period.
