Emanuel Bowen

A Map of the King of Great Britain's Dominions in Europe, Africa, and America

c. 1740 · London

Produced on the eve of the French and Indian War, this map makes British imperial claim its explicit subject. Yellow outlining identifies territories possessed by the King of Great Britain across the Atlantic world.

The Atlantic is framed as a British lake — a statement of what Britain believed it owned, and was prepared to defend. At the moment of maximum tension before open conflict, Bowen's map captures an empire asserting itself across three continents simultaneously.

Emanuel Bowen was Engraver of Maps to both King George II of Great Britain and Louis XV of France — a remarkable dual appointment that speaks to his status as the preeminent cartographer of his era.
