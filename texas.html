Iron Mountain Route to All Parts of Texas

1909 · Houston, Texas

A railroad promotion map advertising round-trip homeseeker rates on the Iron Mountain Route, published for The Texas Realty Journal.

The map annotates the state by agricultural product and land use — cotton, corn, cattle, oil fields — making it as much an economic argument as a geographic document. A vivid artifact of the era's land promotion culture.

At 28,308 × 26,543 pixels, this is the largest image in the collection. Zoom in to read individual county names, rail lines, and crop annotations across the full state.
